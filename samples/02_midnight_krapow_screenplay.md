# Midnight Krapow — Screenplay

Genre: Action-Comedy / Solo Performance
Setting: Small studio apartment, late at night
Actor: 1 (plays themselves and voice-acts a few characters for effect)
Runtime: 3 minutes

---

**[INT. SMALL APARTMENT – NIGHT]**

Dimly lit room. A digital clock reads 2:47 AM. The room is quiet — too quiet. A man (30s), wearing a worn-out delivery uniform, stumbles in, exhausted.

MAN (to himself)
That's it. No more Grab. No more tips. Just… one last Krapow.

He opens his fridge. Empty — except for a chili, an egg, and some holy basil.

MAN (mocking himself)
"Buy groceries," I said. "You'll thank yourself," I said. Idiot.

He pulls out a small pan, lights the stove, and tosses in ingredients. The sizzle is LOUD. Camera zooms in. Everything slows down. His face changes — like he's transforming.

MAN (V.O., deeper tone)
They thought I was done.
But the pan… had other plans.

Suddenly, the chili explodes in the pan — not literally, but with a fiery sound effect. He flinches. The lights flicker. A red glow illuminates the room. He looks down — steam rises from the Krapow like smoke from a superhero origin scene.

MAN (V.O.)
Infused with spice. Forged by hunger.
I became… the Krapow Hero.

He stands heroically. His apron flaps like a cape from the fan's breeze. He holds a spatula like a weapon. Dramatic music kicks in — from his phone, which he taps.

MAN (serious tone)
This city doesn't know it yet…
But tonight, I deliver justice.

He suddenly freezes. Looks around. Realizes he's just talking to himself.

MAN (breaking character)
…I seriously need sleep.

He turns off the stove. The glow fades. He plates the food.

MAN (smiles)
But first — one last Krapow.

He sits, takes a bite. A beat of silence. Then his eyes go wide — too spicy.

MAN (gasping)
Oh my god…
This Krapow is possessed.

He fans his mouth. Lights flicker again. Behind him, the pan rattles mysteriously on the stove…

CUT TO BLACK.
