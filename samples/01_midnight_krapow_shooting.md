# Midnight Krapow — Shooting Script

Genre: Action-Comedy
Runtime: 3 minutes
Camera: Sony A7cii
Actor: One
Setting: Small apartment, night

---

## SCENE 1 — "The Return"
(0:00–0:30)

Location: Apartment Entrance
Lighting: Dim yellow/blue hue (simulate late night)

**SHOT 1** (Wide shot from inside the room)
The door creaks open.
MAN enters, slouched, bag over shoulder.
He sighs deeply, kicks off his shoes.

**SHOT 2** (Close-up)
He drops keys on the counter.
Looks at the clock: 2:47 AM.
SFX: Faint traffic noise outside.

DIALOGUE (to himself):
"That's it. No more Grab. No more tips. Just… one last Krapow."

---

## SCENE 2 — "Fridge of Fate"
(0:30–0:50)

**SHOT 3** (Over-the-shoulder into fridge)
Opens fridge. Empty — only chili, egg, basil.

DIALOGUE (sarcastic):
"'Buy groceries,' I said. 'You'll thank yourself,' I said…"

---

## SCENE 3 — "The Ritual Begins"
(0:50–1:20)

**SHOT 4** (Medium shot, side view)
Lights the stove. Tosses chili into hot pan.
SFX: LOUD SIZZLE

**SHOT 5** (Close-up of his face as red light flickers from the stove)
He flinches. Looks slowly at the pan.

V.O. (deeper, dramatic):
"They thought I was done.
But the pan… had other plans."

---

## SCENE 4 — "Transformation"
(1:20–1:50)

**SHOT 6** (Low-angle shot)
He lifts a spatula like a sword. Dramatic fan wind on apron.
MUSIC CUE: Epic heroic background music from phone.

V.O. (heroic):
"Infused with spice. Forged by hunger…
I became… the Krapow Hero."

---

## SCENE 5 — "Reality Hits"
(1:50–2:15)

**SHOT 7** (Sudden silence — wide shot)
Music cuts. He freezes.

DIALOGUE (normal voice):
"…I seriously need sleep."

He turns off the stove, casually plates the Krapow.

---

## SCENE 6 — "The Bite of Destiny"
(2:15–2:45)

**SHOT 8** (Close-up)
He takes a bite. Pauses. Then —
Eyes widen. Mouth on fire.
SFX: Cartoonish whoosh + fake flame overlay optional

DIALOGUE (panicking):
"Oh my god… This Krapow is possessed!"

---

## SCENE 7 — "Final Flicker"
(2:45–3:00)

**SHOT 9** (Over-the-shoulder, toward stove)
As he fans his mouth, the pan behind him rattles by itself.
Lighting flickers.
He doesn't notice.

CUT TO BLACK.

TEXT ON SCREEN: THE LEGEND BEGINS…

END.
