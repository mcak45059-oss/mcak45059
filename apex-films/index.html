FAQ

What's the turnaround?

First cut in 48 hours from receiving footage. Revisions in 24 hours. If we miss it, the next month is free.

How do revisions work?

Leave timestamped notes in Frame.io or a Loom. Unlimited rounds, no per-edit fees. We don't stop until it's right.

Where do I send footage?

Whatever's easiest — Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io, WeTransfer. We handle ingest, proxies, and storage.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes. Pause any month, cancel any time, no contracts. Founding-member rate is locked in for as long as you stay subscribed.

Who owns the footage and the cuts?

You do. Full ownership of every deliverable, project file included on request.
